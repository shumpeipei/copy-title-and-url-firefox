# Privacy Policy

**Copy Title and URL** does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data or browsing activity.

## Data Processing
- When you activate the extension (via the toolbar button or keyboard shortcut), it accesses the active tab's title and URL strictly within your local browser.
- The formatted text (`<Title>\n<URL>`) is copied directly into your system clipboard via standard WebExtension APIs.
- No network requests are made, and no analytics or telemetry services are integrated.
- No information ever leaves your device.
